## Phase 1 art-lock acceptance gate
- [ ] User reviews puppy design **in context** on both dark and light wallpaper.
- [ ] Artist-quality silhouette, foot anatomy, expressions, tail and fabric-free outline approved.
- [ ] 3/4 front, side, rear/turn views visually consistent and authored as independently controllable layers.
- [ ] Distinct walking contact/passing/stopping/turning, idle, talking, Done, Snooze poses exist in source form.
- [ ] Exported layers display without seam gaps at 64, 160, 240px.
- [ ] Motion study accepted at 0.25x playback with no foot skating or jarring cuts.
- [ ] Release never blocked by missing pet art; fallback exists.

**Do not call Phase 1 visually locked while the unchecked items remain.** The branch intentionally isolates early quality exploration and protects existing accepted static pets. The product is Windows-first; Android work in other branches is not modified.
